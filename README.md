# RECUR Corpus

The **RECUR Corpus** is a German clinical text corpus derived from the RECUR registry and developed for research in medical natural language processing (NLP).

This repository provides information about the corpus, its controlled-access procedure, the Data Usage Agreement (DUA), and the required citation.

## Dataset Access

Due to the sensitive nature of the underlying clinical data, the RECUR Corpus is not publicly downloadable. The corpus is made available to eligible researchers through a controlled-access procedure under a **Data Usage Agreement (DUA)**.

### Eligibility

The RECUR Corpus may be requested for **non-commercial scientific research** by researchers affiliated with recognized academic or research institutions.

Access is granted to individual researchers for a specified research purpose and is subject to institutional approval.

### How to Request Access

To request access to the RECUR Corpus:

1. Download and read the **RECUR Data Usage Agreement (DUA)**.
2. Complete the DUA with your name, institutional affiliation, position, contact information, and a short description of the intended research purpose.
3. Sign the agreement and have it signed by the person responsible for the secure administration of the system on which the RECUR Corpus will be stored.
4. Send the completed agreement to the institutional contact listed below.
5. Following approval, instructions for accessing the corpus will be provided to the applicant.

**[Download the RECUR Data Usage Agreement](RECUR_DUA.pdf)**

Submission of an application does not automatically constitute approval.

## Conditions of Use

Use of the RECUR Corpus is governed by the RECUR Data Usage Agreement.

In particular, Data Users agree to:

- use the corpus only for the approved non-commercial research purpose;
- store the corpus securely and prevent unauthorized access;
- not distribute, disclose, or otherwise share the corpus with third parties;
- not attempt to identify or re-identify individuals represented in the corpus;
- not attempt to reconstruct original clinical documents from the corpus;
- comply with the usage period specified in the DUA; and
- cite the RECUR Corpus publication in scientific work based on the corpus.

The complete conditions are specified in the **RECUR Data Usage Agreement**.
